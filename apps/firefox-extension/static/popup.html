    YOLO applies to every agent connected to this browser, including passwords, submits and sends, and stays on until you switch it off. STOP still halts everything. Every decision is saved in History.

    Agent window

    The agent opens its own window the first time it navigates and keeps its pages there. It can still use one of your tabs if it selects it on purpose. "Take over" stops the agent and shows its window so you can finish by hand; "Hand back" lets it continue. The window shares your logins: it is not a separate account or a sandbox.

    Record me

    Saved only on this computer, never sent anywhere else. Passwords, card numbers and one-time codes are never recorded, and private windows are never recorded. It captures your clicks, selections and typed fields in the main page of a tab. The toolbar shows REC while it is on.
